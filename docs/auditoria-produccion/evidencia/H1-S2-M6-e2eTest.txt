## Causa raiz (aislada a mano con docker run, misma config que KafkaHelper de testcontainers 1.21.3)
- KAFKA_ADVERTISED_LISTENERS=PLAINTEXT://localhost:9092,BROKER://kafka:9093              -> mismo error (advertised 0.0.0.0)
- KAFKA_ADVERTISED_LISTENERS=PLAINTEXT://localhost:9092,BROKER://kafka:9093,CONTROLLER://kafka:9094
  -> "[BrokerLifecycleManager id=1] The broker has been unfenced. Transitioning from RECOVERY to RUNNING."
Kafka 3.9.0 exige (observado arriba, no tomado de la doc) anunciar CONTROLLER en modo broker+controller; KafkaContainer de
testcontainers 1.21.x solo anuncia PLAINTEXT y BROKER. Clase: TEST_BUG (imagen de prueba incompatible
con la version de testcontainers), no ENVIRONMENT. Un intento previo con hostname explicito
(withCreateContainerCmdModifier) NO lo resolvio y se revirtio.
